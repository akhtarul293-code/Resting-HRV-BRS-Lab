Methods & interpretation

R peaks are detected from a moving-baseline ECG derivative-energy signal and can be corrected in the ECG plot. Each BP beat is measured in an R-to-R window shifted by the chosen pulse delay. MAP is the mean pressure of samples within that beat; PP = SBP − DBP. HRV time-domain metrics use accepted NN intervals; successive differences never cross a rejected beat. SBP ARV uses consecutive accepted pairs. Frequency-domain HRV uses 4 Hz interpolation, linear detrending and a Hann periodogram, and is suppressed when fewer than 95% of beats are accepted or a gap exceeds 5 s. Spontaneous sequence BRS pairs SBP with the following RR interval, requires at least three paired values, matching trends, the configured step thresholds and Pearson r, and reports mean regression slope in ms/mmHg.

This is a research review tool. Confirm peak timing, BP alignment, calibration and artifact handling before reporting findings. BRS sequence slope does not establish reflex causality.
